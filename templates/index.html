What is GIC?

Global Identity Card (GIC) Private Limited provides verified digital identities to individuals worldwide. Our mission is rooted in the values of Justice, Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity, aligned with the Indian Constitution and international human rights principles.

Our Core Commitments

  • Secure Verification: All documents are confidential and securely encrypted.
  • IP Protection: All contributions during engagement remain company-owned.
  • Revenue-Based Hiring: No salary until monetization, with performance incentives encouraged.
  • Remote-First: 100% online workflows supporting global collaboration.
  • Transparency: All members must disclose conflicts and avoid platform misuse.
  • Data Integrity: GIC securely stores and manages all verified identity data.

Our Ethical Framework

  • Indian Constitutional Values: Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity
  • Global Human Rights: Respecting every identity fairly
  • Company Ethics: Non-discrimination, transparency, and security-first culture
